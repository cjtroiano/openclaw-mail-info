OpenClaw Mail

Gmail read-and-send assistance for a single account owner, operated through a private, self-hosted OpenClaw assistant.

Overview Privacy policy Contact the operator

Authorization happens separately

Mailbox access requires the owner's separate Google authorization and a private runtime connection. Publishing or visiting this website does not authorize mail access or enable sending. This site does not claim Google verification, approval or full policy compliance; the privacy policy describes the deployment's data-protection limitations.

What the integration is for

OpenClaw Mail is intended to help its account owner read and summarize messages, identify correspondence that needs attention, and compose and send email through the owner's designated Gmail inbox.

Once the owner authorizes and enables the integration, the assistant is intended to handle ordinary correspondence autonomously under the owner's standing instructions. It is not designed to ask for approval before every message it sends.

This is a private, account-owner integration, not a public email service. There is no public signup, shared inbox access, or invitation to connect a visitor's Google account. The operator's primary personal Gmail account is outside its intended scope.

Intended Gmail access

The selected Gmail data scopes are limited to:

  • Read email (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly) to read messages and their associated information for the owner's requests and standing instructions.
  • Send email (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send) to send correspondence on the owner's behalf.

The client also requests OpenID/email identity permissions to verify which Google account granted access. Those permissions do not add Calendar, Drive or other Google data services.

No Calendar or Drive access is requested. Deleting messages and managing Gmail settings are not part of the selected permissions.

How mail information may be handled

When Gmail access is enabled, the private OpenClaw runtime may process message content and associated information to perform these tasks. Mail content may be sent to configured AI inference providers and may be retained in self-hosted conversations or workspaces.

Before Gmail data is sent to an AI provider, the operator must verify compatible provider-use and data controls. Google user data must not be used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. The privacy policy separately discloses the deployment's retained-storage encryption limitation.

Read the privacy policy for the intended data use, storage, sharing, access controls, and how to ask about deletion or revoke access.

About this website

This static site provides information about the integration and its privacy practices. It does not sign you in, accept credentials, handle OAuth callbacks, or display mailbox data. There is no backend or interactive Gmail connection on this site.

The site does not include analytics scripts, external fonts, or third-party embedded content. GitHub Pages, the site hosting provider, may collect visitor IP addresses and security-related information as part of operating its service.
